# 🏟️ Sports Footwear Sales Analysis - Tableau Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Tableau dashboard built on a realistic global sports footwear sales dataset from 2018 to 2026.  
The goal is to transform raw transactional data into meaningful business insights related to sales performance, pricing strategy, customer behavior, and market trends.

The dashboard is designed to reflect real-world retail analytics use cases commonly used by business intelligence teams.

---

## 📂 Dataset Description

The dataset contains 30,000+ sales records covering major sportswear brands:

Nike, Adidas, Puma, Reebok, ASICS, New Balance

### Key Columns

- order_id – Unique transaction ID  
- order_date – Date of sale  
- brand – Shoe brand  
- model_name – Product model  
- category – Running, Training, Basketball, Lifestyle, Gym  
- gender – Men, Women, Unisex  
- size – Shoe size  
- color – Shoe color  
- base_price_usd – Original price  
- discount_percent – Discount applied  
- final_price_usd – Price after discount  
- units_sold – Quantity sold  
- revenue_usd – Total revenue  
- payment_method – Card, Cash, Wallet, Bank Transfer  
- sales_channel – Online or Retail Store  
- country – Country of sale  
- customer_income_level – Low, Medium, High  
- customer_rating – Rating from 3.0 to 5.0

## 📊 Dashboard Highlights

### Sales Analysis
- Revenue and units sold over time

### Pricing & Discount Impact
- Relationship between discounts and revenue

### Customer Insights
- Sales by income group  
- Customer rating trends  
- Payment method behavior  

### Channel Performance
- Online vs retail sales comparison  

All visuals include interactive filters for deeper exploration.
